quorum. component specimens

The seven components the requirements name, drawn from the library rather than from a mock. Nothing on this page picks a colour, a size or a hue: every value is a token and every phase colour comes from the phase name.

Participants

identity chip · hue is keyed on (name, harness), so it survives a reconnect · a human gets a square dot and --id-human · status="waiting" pulses: blocked in wait_for_events

message row · challenge draws the coral rail · system and claim rows are mono and unattributed · the private refusal carries no seq, because no event carried it

Deliberation

phase stepper · Failed replaces the terminal step — the rail never shows a path not taken, and never grows a fifth box

proposal card · options are listed and the tally is not — during voting a visible count is the anchoring hidden ballots exist to prevent

vote chip · a ballot, a recorded vote, or a tally row

dissent badge · violet, never red: dissent is a record, not an error · never truncated, never behind a "show more"

Records

decision record · the one light surface: warm paper, serif, a real shadow · ink is the live present, paper is the permanent past

failure record · failure is an outcome with a typed reason (protocol D8), on the same paper · everyone who never cast is named, because "3 of 6 ballots" is not a record of what happened
